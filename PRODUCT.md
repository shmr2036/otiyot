## Platform

Static single-file Hebrew page (`index.html`, `lang="he" dir="rtl"`), no build step. Mobile traffic is expected to dominate because the page is shared through WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Users

- **Primary:** women considering a one-day writing retreat. The group and the staff are women only. Many come alone (the FAQ answers "I'm afraid to come alone" directly).
- **Who the copy speaks to:** a woman "who was burned once or twice, not consumed but forged, whose ember still glows under the ash and wants to burn again."
- **Their job on this page:** decide whether this day is for her, trust Emily enough to commit, and pay (or ask a question first).
- **Arrival context (inferred):** a link opened on a phone from WhatsApp or Instagram, often forwarded by a friend or from Emily's own channels.

## Product Purpose

"מעשה אותיות" (Ma'aseh Otiyot) is a one-day women-only writing retreat, a "ריטריטון", led by writer Emily Amrusi (אמילי עמרוסי). It uses writing as a dialogue with the subconscious and the superconscious: "we don't learn to write, we learn to know ourselves through writing."

Success for this page: registrations paid through the invoice4u payment page, with early-bird sign-ups before 30/11; WhatsApp conversations with the coordinator are the secondary success path.

## Positioning

- Emily's own method and voice: handwritten, focused exercises with teaching segments, no obligation to share. It is self-discovery through writing, not a writing-craft course.
- A compressed, single-day sister to Emily's multi-day retreat "חלון לרוח" (brand "אמילים — אמנות במילים", https://emilim.net/). It offers the same depth in one day ("יום אחד מרוכז").
- It is anchored in a specific date and place: the 5th candle of Hanukkah at Khan Metzokei Dragot above the Dead Sea. The fire and light theme of Hanukkah ties into the retreat's fire and ember language.
- It is women-only end to end, participants and staff alike.

## Product Principles

1. **Emily's voice is the product.** The page should read as her speaking to one woman, in the first person, in feminine Hebrew. Copy is not to be neutralised into marketing register.
2. **One clear act: say "I'm with you."** Every primary action leads to the same payment page. WhatsApp is there for the hesitant, not as an equal alternative.
3. **Safety before intensity.** The fire language invites, and the facts reassure: women only, no obligation to share, coming alone is fine, everything is included.
4. **True and specific.** Date, place, price, and program are concrete and exact. Nothing is invented: testimonials come only from the approved חלון לרוח set, and urgency is limited to the real 30/11 early-bird deadline and the confirmed seat limit.
5. **Phone-first, shared-link-first.** The page must make its case on a phone opened from WhatsApp or Instagram.
